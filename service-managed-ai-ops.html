Managed AI Ops – End‑to‑End AI Operations for Enterprise

Enterprises that scale AI quickly discover that building and maintaining the operational backbone—monitoring, model drift detection, incident response, and compliance—requires a dedicated, expert team. Salyant’s Managed AI Ops service provides that team as a fully outsourced function, allowing you to focus on business outcomes while we keep your AI models performant, secure, and aligned with regulatory expectations.
Key Takeaway for Decision Makers

What does a Managed AI Ops service include for enterprise organisations?

A Managed AI Ops service provides continuous model monitoring, automated drift detection, performance optimisation, incident management, governance, and 24/7 technical support—all delivered under defined service‑level agreements (SLAs) and tiered support options.

Grounding Evidence: Industry benchmarks from Gartner and IDC show that enterprises with dedicated AI Ops teams achieve up to 30 % higher model reliability and 20 % lower operational cost. Leading managed‑AI providers publish tiered SLAs (e.g., 99.5 % uptime, 2‑hour critical incident response) that are now standard expectations for enterprise‑grade services.

Why Managed AI Ops Matters

AI models degrade over time as data distributions shift, infrastructure changes, or regulatory requirements evolve. Without proactive operations, hidden drift can cause inaccurate predictions, compliance breaches, and costly re‑training cycles.

A managed approach embeds continuous observability, automated remediation, and expert oversight into the AI lifecycle, turning AI from a project risk into a reliable production capability.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Managed AI Ops differ from traditional MLOps tooling?

Traditional MLOps tools provide the pipeline to build and deploy models. Managed AI Ops adds a continuous operations layer—monitoring, drift remediation, compliance, and 24/7 incident response—delivered by a dedicated team under contractual SLAs.

Can Managed AI Ops be applied to on‑premise AI deployments?

Yes. Our service integrates with on‑prem Kubernetes clusters, private clouds, and hybrid environments, installing lightweight agents that feed telemetry back to our secure operations hub.

What happens if a critical incident exceeds the SLA response time?

All tiers include financial service credits proportional to the breach duration, as defined in the contract. Enterprise Tier customers also receive a post‑mortem analysis and remediation plan at no additional cost.

Do you support model retraining as part of the managed service?

Automated retraining triggers are part of the core offering. For custom retraining strategies or large‑scale batch jobs, we provide a consult‑to‑execute model that can be scoped as an add‑on.

How is data privacy handled when monitoring models that use sensitive data?

Telemetry is stripped of personally identifiable information before transmission. All data in transit is encrypted with TLS 1.3, and at rest is stored in ISO‑27001‑certified vaults within the UK or EU, per your data‑residency requirements.
