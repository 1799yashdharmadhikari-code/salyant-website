An independent audit satisfies both: it produces board-ready evidence of controlled innovation and identifies high-confidence automation levers.

Four-Week Audit Methodology

Week 1: Stakeholder workshops (COO, CTO, CISO, Data Lead, Business Unit Heads) to define scope, success criteria, and access requirements. Automated discovery scans SaaS estate, code repos, and data catalogues for undeclared AI components.

Week 2: Deep-dive assessment of 8-12 prioritised processes. Evaluation covers data pipelines, model performance, human-in-the-loop design, monitoring coverage, and vendor contract terms. Each use case scored on feasibility, value, risk, and time-to-value.

Week 3: Financial modelling workshop with finance team. Build bottom-up business cases: compute costs, licence fees, internal effort, risk mitigation spend, and expected benefit realisation profile. Sensitivity analysis on adoption rates and model drift.

Week 4: Board-ready deliverables presentation. Includes executive summary, detailed findings register, prioritised roadmap with 90/180/360-day milestones, resource plan, and governance framework proposal. Optional: implementation kick-off sprint planning.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does this differ from a Big 4 AI readiness assessment?

Big 4 deliver compliance-heavy slide decks. We deliver executable engineering plans. Our architects have built and operated production AI systems in regulated UK environments—banks, insurers, manufacturers. The audit output is a prioritised backlog with technical specs, not a maturity model scorecard.

What access do you need from our team?

Read-only access to SaaS admin consoles (Microsoft 365, Salesforce, ServiceNow, Databricks, Snowflake), code repositories, data catalogues, and vendor contracts. Plus 4-6 hours of stakeholder workshops across Week 1-2. No production data extraction required.

Can you audit specific business units rather than whole enterprise?

Yes. We scope to a division, geography, or function (e.g., finance transformation, supply chain, customer operations). Minimum viable scope: 3-5 core processes with accessible data owners. Fixed price adjusts proportionally.

What if we already have an AI strategy document?

We stress-test it. Most strategies lack technical feasibility validation, vendor negotiation leverage, and regulatory mapping. Our audit either validates your roadmap with evidence or redirects investment before sunk cost accumulates.

How do you handle sensitive IP and data during discovery?

All discovery runs in your environment via containerised tooling. No data leaves your boundary. We operate under mutual NDA and ISO 27001-aligned handling procedures. UK data residency guaranteed.
