Delivery model

How the portfolio programme works

01 · Baseline

Map the portfolio

Agree the company and domain inventory, target markets, business objectives, existing analytics access and the right level of diagnostic depth. Establish a baseline for each included website rather than blending all sites into one score.

02 · Prioritise

Build a value-led backlog

Compare technical issues, content gaps, answer clarity, generative-search context, dependencies and delivery readiness. Rank work by agreed business relevance, effort and risk—not by a generic checklist or an assumed search ranking.

03 · Govern and implement

Run company-level workstreams

Define an owner, scope, access permissions, approval route and verification method for each work package. Changes are implemented only under agreed permissions; unattended publishing is not implied.

04 · Report and reprioritise

Give the operating partner a usable view

Provide a monthly portfolio summary alongside company-level scorecards, completed work, open risks, dependencies and next priorities. Measures and cadence are agreed at onboarding and distinguished from outcomes outside Salyant’s control.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a generic SEO package applied to every portfolio company?

No. The portfolio view is consistent, but each company receives its own baseline, priorities, workstream, owner and approvals.

Can the work be coordinated across several companies?

Yes. Company-level workstreams can be coordinated under a portfolio operating model, subject to agreed capacity, access and decision rights.

Does the programme change portfolio websites automatically?

No unattended publishing is implied. Production changes follow agreed access, scope, approval and verification controls.

Can Salyant guarantee rankings or AI citations?

No. Search engines and AI systems control crawling, ranking, selection and citations. Reporting focuses on observable work and agreed measures.
