Frequently Asked Questions

No. Salyant operates as a fully managed service—we handle everything from model training and deployment to daily monitoring and optimization. Your team focuses on strategy and business outcomes while we execute the technical delivery.

Our service includes continuous monitoring and automatic alerting when performance deviates from baseline. If drift occurs, we immediately deploy a retraining pipeline using fresh, high-quality data to restore optimal performance.
