Automated SEO, AEO & GEO Growth Engine

Search visibility now spans three fronts at once: classic Google rankings (SEO), the answers ChatGPT and Perplexity give about your business (AEO), and how generative engines summarise and cite you (GEO). Manual retainers can't keep pace with all three simultaneously. Salyant deploys the same autonomous growth engine we run on our own site — a daily pipeline that audits, benchmarks against live competitors, and publishes fixes without waiting for a monthly review cycle.
Key Takeaway for Decision Makers

How do I keep my site visible across Google, ChatGPT and AI search at the same time?

Salyant installs a managed automation pipeline on your site that runs a full technical, AEO and GEO audit daily, crawls the competitors currently winning your target queries, and publishes the single highest-impact fix each cycle — a meta fix, an FAQ block, a new service page, or a structured-data update — subject to safety gates you control.

Grounding Evidence: This is the identical architecture powering salyant.co.uk's own growth engine, in production since August 2026, with every publish action logged and scored against measurable before/after outcomes.

Why One Engine Instead of Three Separate Efforts

Most agencies still treat SEO, AEO and GEO as three separate line items, each with its own tooling and its own monthly report. In practice they draw on the same underlying signals: structured data, clear answer blocks, crawlable content, and genuine topical authority. Running them as one engine means every fix compounds across all three channels instead of being optimised in isolation.

The engine treats your site as a living system: it reads your current pages, maps internal links, checks for orphaned content, and cross-references that against what competitors are doing right now — not what they were doing when a report was last commissioned.

  • One pipeline covering technical SEO, AEO answer blocks, and GEO structured data
  • Daily competitor crawl instead of a quarterly audit
  • Every fix scored, logged, and tied to a measurable before/after
  • No duplicated tooling or reporting across three vendors

How the Growth Engine Works

Each cycle, the engine pulls your current site content, audits it against technical, AEO and GEO checklists, and generates a set of high-intent buyer queries relevant to your market. It searches for who is currently winning those queries, crawls their pages for structure, proof points, and pricing signals, and builds a scored opportunity queue ranked by impact, confidence and effort.

The single highest-scoring action for that day — whether that's a meta tag rewrite, a new FAQ block with schema, a dedicated service page, or a structured-data injection — is generated, checked against a design-fidelity gate that verifies it matches your site's real branding and legal details, and only then published. Every action is deduplicated against fatigued patterns so the engine doesn't repeat the same fix on a loop.

  • Daily technical, AEO and GEO audit of your live site
  • Automated competitor query generation and multi-page crawling
  • Scored opportunity queue, ranked by impact and confidence
  • Design-fidelity and claims-safety gate before anything publishes

Governance: You Control What Goes Live

Autonomous publishing makes people nervous, reasonably so. The engine is built with explicit safety rails: a minimum opportunity-confidence threshold before anything ships, a rejection path that alerts a human reviewer instead of publishing when confidence is low, and a hard block on unverifiable claims (no invented client names, no "100% guaranteed" language).

For clients who prefer a human in the loop, we configure the engine to draft and queue changes for approval rather than publish directly — you get the same daily research and prioritisation, with a manual gate before anything reaches your live site.

  • Configurable autonomy: full auto-publish or approval-gated
  • Every commit and rejection logged with a reason
  • Automated rollback path if a change fails post-publish verification
  • No fabricated testimonials, guarantees, or statistics

Engagement Model

Engagements start with a one-week setup: connecting your site's repository or CMS, defining your target buyer queries and no-go topics, and running the engine in shadow mode (scoring opportunities without publishing) so you can see exactly what it would have done before it goes live.

From there, the engine runs on a daily schedule with weekly reporting on what was published, what was rejected, and the resulting score trend across technical, AEO and GEO dimensions.

  • Week 1: connect repo/CMS, define target queries, shadow-mode run
  • Week 2 onward: live daily cycle with your chosen autonomy level
  • Weekly score trend report across technical, AEO and GEO
  • Monthly strategy review to adjust target queries and clusters

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an SEO, AEO and GEO growth engine?

It is an autonomous workflow that audits your site daily, benchmarks it against live competitors, and publishes fixes and new content automatically — covering traditional search (SEO), answer engines like ChatGPT and Perplexity (AEO), and generative AI search (GEO).
