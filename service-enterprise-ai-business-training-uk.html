Delivery Model: Workshop, Assessment, Advisory

Training without application evaporates. Every Salyant engagement combines structured learning with live work on your actual automation pipeline — ensuring immediate application and measurable progression.

Phase 1: Diagnostic Workshop (Day 1) — We map your current AI maturity, opportunity backlog, and organisational readiness against our Enterprise AI Maturity Model. Output: prioritised capability gaps and tailored curriculum emphasis.

Phase 2: Structured Learning (Days 2–5) — Role-specific modules delivered on-site or at our London labs. Combines frameworks, case studies from UK enterprise deployments, and hands-on exercises using your systems and data (under NDA).

Phase 3: Live Portfolio Assessment (Week 2) — Your teams apply frameworks to 3–5 live opportunities under architect supervision. Output: validated business cases, risk registers, and implementation roadmaps ready for investment committee review.

Phase 4: 90-Day Advisory Retainer — Monthly checkpoints, template library access, and architect office hours to embed capability and course-correct early initiatives.

  • Diagnostic workshop establishes baseline and customises curriculum
  • Live portfolio assessment converts learning into investment-ready artefacts
  • 90-day advisory retainer ensures capability embeds, not evaporates
  • Delivered by practicing Salyant architects, not professional trainers

Frequently Asked Questions

How does this differ from Microsoft, AWS, or Google AI certifications?

Vendor certifications teach platform features. We teach vendor-agnostic evaluation frameworks, governance models, and architecture patterns that apply across any technology stack — enabling your teams to challenge vendor proposals rather than implement them uncritically.

Can training be delivered remotely for distributed UK teams?

Yes. We deliver via secure virtual labs with identical curriculum, live portfolio assessment, and architect office hours. On-site delivery at our London labs or your offices is available for cohorts over 12 participants.

What prerequisites do participants need?

Executive Track: P&L or investment authority. Technical Track: Hands-on experience with data pipelines, APIs, or cloud infrastructure. Delivery Track: Process analysis or business analyst experience. We calibrate depth in the diagnostic workshop.

How do you measure training ROI?

We agree success metrics pre-engagement: opportunity pipeline quality score (validated vs raw), time-to-investment-committee, external consultant dependency reduction, and deployment success rate at 6 and 12 months. Results tracked via shared dashboard.

Is this eligible for Apprenticeship Levy or UK skills funding?

Our Licensed Curriculum model aligns with Level 7 Senior Leader Apprenticeship standards and can be mapped to eligible programmes. We provide curriculum mapping documentation for your training provider or levy administrator.
