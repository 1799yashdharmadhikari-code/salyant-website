Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to making this website usable by as many people as possible, and how to tell us if something isn't working for you.

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Our commitment

Salyant Ltd wants this website to be usable by as many people as possible, including people using assistive technology such as screen readers, magnification, or keyboard-only navigation.

Standards we aim for

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. This is an ongoing goal rather than a certified conformance claim — we haven't yet commissioned a formal third-party accessibility audit of this Site.

Measures we've taken

  • Semantic HTML headings and landmarks throughout each page.
  • A keyboard-operable navigation menu, including a mobile menu with proper aria-expanded and aria-hidden states.
  • Descriptive link text and alt text on meaningful images.
  • A colour palette chosen for reasonable contrast against its dark background.

Known limitations

Some third-party embeds on this Site — including the Cal.com booking widget — are built and maintained outside Salyant, so their accessibility depends on that provider. We review these where practical but can't guarantee they meet the same standard as our own markup.

Feedback

If you find any part of this Site difficult to use, please tell us — we want to fix it. Email contact@salyant.co.uk with the page and the issue you ran into, and we'll aim to respond promptly.
